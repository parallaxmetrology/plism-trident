Lab book · TRIDENT colour-profile rerun · built by scripts/04_labbook_icc.py

TRIDENT on paired PLISM scans, with each scan's embedded colour profile applied

Same 26 scans, TRIDENT commit, weights, settings, fields and scoring as the original TRIDENT study (../trident-plism). One change: every region TRIDENT reads is converted from the scan's embedded profile ("AT2" or "Segue_v2") to sRGB. Applied, the profiles take Otsu from 28 to 4 switched fields of 156, GrandQC from 1 to 0 and HEST from 6 to 0. HEST with artifact removal goes from 26 to 53.

With the profile applied, the artifact step keeps a smaller share of AT2 tissue than in the original run on 12 of 13 sections; HR is the exception, where it keeps more on both scanners. GT450's share moves by at most 8.2 percentage points from its original value except on HR. The result is a GT450 region larger than the AT2 region on 13 of 13 sections, and 47 of the 53 switched fields are admitted on GT450 only. HEST alone switches 0 fields under the same conversion, so the added switches come from the artifact step. For reference, on 2000-pixel OpenSlide thumbnails with the profiles applied, AT2 tissue is brighter than GT450 tissue on 13 of 13 sections (median tissue luma; ../plism-icc/thumbnail_check.json); without them it is darker on all 13.

7. Scope

Same array, scanners, software and settings; only the colour conversion changes. One rendering intent. Thirteen serial, differently stained sections of one mixed-tissue TMA; two scanners from one vendor. Descriptive: it does not say which region is right. The segmentation and artifact models were trained on images whose colour handling is not known here, so converted pixels are not assumed to be closer to their training data.
